Next Up – Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Summary

Next Up does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The app runs on your phone and talks only to Spotify, using your own Spotify account. Gham Studios runs no servers for this app, and the app contains no ads, analytics or trackers.

This policy covers the Next Up Android app (com.ghamstudios.nextup). Next Up is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify AB.

Information the app handles

Next Up handles the following, and keeps it on your device:

  • Spotify sign-in tokens. When you tap Connect Spotify, you log in on Spotify’s own web page. Next Up never sees your Spotify password. It receives an access token and a refresh token, which let it read your playback.
  • Playback information. While announcing is on, the app asks Spotify for your current playback state (the song playing, its progress, whether it is paused, and whether it comes from a playlist) and your play queue, to find the next song and artist. Song and artist names are held in memory only long enough to speak them and show the status line. They are not saved.
  • App settings. Your lead-time and playlist-only choices are saved on your device.

Next Up does not access your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone, and does not read your list of playlists or use your device’s advertising ID.

How information is used and shared

Next Up uses this information for one purpose: to announce the next song and artist aloud shortly before the current song ends. Gham Studios does not receive, store or sell it, and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

The app sends data to only two kinds of services:

  • Spotify. With your access token, the app sends requests to Spotify’s servers to sign you in and read your playback. Spotify’s own privacy policy, on Spotify’s website, covers how Spotify handles that data.
  • Your device’s text-to-speech engine. The announcement text (for example, “Next up: Song, by Artist”) is passed to the speech engine you chose in Android settings. Some engines work on your device and some use an online service under their own privacy policy. Next Up does not choose or control this.

Storage, security and retention

Everything Next Up stores, the Spotify tokens and your settings, stays in the app’s private storage on your device. Android’s app sandbox and your device’s storage encryption protect it, and the app is excluded from Android cloud backup. All communication with Spotify uses HTTPS.

The data is kept until you disconnect Spotify, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. Access tokens are short-lived (usually one hour) and refresh automatically while you are connected. Playback information is never retained. Because Gham Studios holds none of your data, there is nothing on our side to retain, breach or hand over.

Your choices

You control what Next Up can do at any time:

  • Stop announcing. Tap Stop announcing in the app, or Stop in the notification.
  • Disconnect Spotify. Tap Disconnect Spotify in the app. This stops announcements and deletes the stored tokens from your device.
  • Revoke access at Spotify. Remove Next Up from the Apps section of your Spotify account settings. This cancels its permission even if the app is still installed.
  • Erase everything. Clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to remove your settings too.

Gham Studios holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. For data Spotify holds about you, contact Spotify.

Permissions

Next Up asks for the minimum Android permissions it needs to work.

PermissionWhy the app needs it
InternetTo sign in to Spotify and read your playback and queue.
Notifications (Android 13 and later)To show the ongoing “Next Up is listening” notification with a Stop button. You can decline this.
Foreground service (media playback)To keep announcing while your screen is off or Spotify is in front.

The Spotify sign-in page lists the Spotify permissions Next Up requests: reading your playback state and currently playing track. It cannot play, pause, skip or change anything in your account.

Children, changes and contact

Next Up is not directed at children. Gham Studios collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and Spotify sets its own minimum age for accounts.
